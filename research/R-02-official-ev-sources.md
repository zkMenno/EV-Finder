# R-02 — Official open-data sources for EVs (NL, DE, FR, BE, UK + EU)

## Question

For each launch country (NL, DE, FR, BE, UK), which official bodies publish open data about electric cars (fleet/registrations per make/model/year, new registrations, type-approval specs, catalogue prices, inspection results, charging infrastructure)? How do primary registries relate to the official aggregators that clean them up? We prefer the cleaned official aggregate where it fits, but must know every primary source behind it. Also cover the EU level (EEA, EAFO, ACEA) and how UK DVLA/DfT/DVSA fit.

## Answer (short)

- **NL:** RDW is the primary registry and publishes per-vehicle (per-plate) data under **CC0**. RVO and CBS are aggregators of RDW data. RVO's EV dashboards are **CC BY-SA 4.0**, and share-alike is a red flag. CBS is **CC BY 4.0** but has no make/model. **Use RDW directly** and aggregate it ourselves. Note that RDW's notice says reusers may not state that the data comes from RDW (see gotchas).
- **DE:** KBA is the primary registry and also the only official aggregator. It publishes monthly FZ10 (new registrations by make × model series, with a BEV column) and annual FZ stock series. The licence is **"Datenlizenz Deutschland – Namensnennung – Version 2.0"**, stated inside the files. **Use KBA FZ10, plus FZ2/FZ4/FZ28 for stock.**
- **FR:** The primary registry is SIV (Ministère de l'Intérieur/ANTS) and it is **not open**. The SDES aggregate (Licence Ouverte) has **no make/model**, only fuel × commune × Crit'Air. ADEME Car Labelling (Licence Ouverte) gives make/model specs **and prices** for the current new-car market. There is no official open make/model registration data in FR, so EEA has to fill that gap.
- **BE:** FPS Mobility (SPF Mobilité/FOD Mobiliteit, DIV) is the primary registry. Its open data includes **annual registrations by make and commercial model** (CC0 according to the EU portal metadata). Statbel republishes DIV data. Both websites block automated clients with a captcha.
- **UK:** DVLA is the primary registry. **DfT** is the official aggregator and its make/model/fuel data files (df_VEH0120, df_VEH0160, df_VEH0124, df_VEH0270) are under **OGL v3**. This is the best-structured source of all five countries. DVSA publishes MOT results (bulk files and an API) under OGL. The DVLA VES API works per plate and needs a key, so it is not useful for statistics.
- **EU:** **EEA CO2 cars monitoring** (CC BY 4.0, copyright DG CLIMA) is the only cross-country dataset at make + commercial name + type/variant/version level, with WLTP CO2, Wh/km and electric range. It is filled by national registries (RDW, KBA, SIV, DIV) and is annual, provisional then final. **EAFO** (DG MOVE) falls under the EC CC BY 4.0 legal notice, but its vehicle figures come from third parties, so it needs a check. **ACEA** is not open data.

| Used-market transactions | RDW `datum_tenaamstelling` (🟡, only the latest holder) | none | **SDES immat. occasion** (fuel-level) | FPS Mobility annual (used vs new) | none |
| BEV/PHEV flag | RDW brandstof (single "Elektriciteit" row = BEV) | FZ10 columns | SDES `CARBURANT`; ADEME `Energie` | FPS fuel (monthly) | DfT `Fuel` |
| Specs (WLTP range, Wh/km, power) | **RDW brandstof** (per plate) | **EEA** | **ADEME Car Labelling**; EEA | **EEA** | EEA up to 2020; DfT has none. Gap |
| Catalogue price | **RDW `catalogusprijs`** | none | **ADEME `Prix_véhicule`** (current models only) | none | none |
| Inspection results | **RDW gebreken** | none | none | none | **DVSA MOT bulk** |
| Charging (if needed) | NDW (check) | BNetzA (CC BY 4.0) | IRVE / PAN (Licence Ouverte) | Flanders/Fluvius (check) | DfT stats (check) |

Rule of thumb: prefer the **official aggregator** where it carries make/model (KBA, DfT, EEA). Go **primary** where the aggregator drops make/model or adds a worse licence (NL: RDW over RVO/CBS; FR has no open primary, so use EEA + ADEME).

## Licence red flags

- **RVO dashboards: CC BY-SA 4.0.** Share-alike could force our derived dataset under BY-SA. Use RVO only for cross-checking and ingest RDW instead.
- **RDW CC0 notice:** "bij hergebruik niet toegestaan te vermelden dat de gegevens afkomstig zijn van de RDW" and no RDW logo/house style. This conflicts with our wish to show provenance. It is probably meant as "don't imply endorsement", but the literal text forbids naming RDW. **Needs a decision or an email to RDW** before we show "Source: RDW" in the UI.
- **KBA:** dl-de/by-2-0 requires attribution. The KBA site also asks for prior notice ("nach vorheriger Anzeige") before linking to kba.de and reserves the right to forbid a link.
- **BE (FPS Mobility, Statbel):** CC0 appears only in the EU-portal metadata. The publisher pages are captcha-blocked and the licence is unconfirmed.
- **EAFO:** the EC CC BY 4.0 notice excludes third-party content, and the vehicle and charging data are likely third-party.
