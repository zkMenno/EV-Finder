# Data licence

## Data we publish

Datasets EV Finder publishes openly (real-world range figures, charging stats, used price-band summaries, degradation curves and market counts) are licensed under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

You may copy, share and adapt them for any purpose, including commercially, as long as you give credit. Please credit like this:

> Source: EV Finder (data release `<number>`, `<date>`), CC BY 4.0

and link to the dataset page where you can.

## What this does not cover

- **Third-party data** keeps its own licence. Each source and its required attribution is listed on the site's method page and stored with every fact in the pipeline.
- **ODbL data** (for example OpenStreetMap) is kept separate and never mixed into CC BY datasets.
- **Listing-level data, real-time prices and the paid API** are not open data. They're covered by separate terms.
- **The code** in this repo is under the GNU AGPL-3.0; see [LICENSE](LICENSE).
