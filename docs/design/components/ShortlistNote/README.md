# ShortlistNote

The hand-kept list: your shortlist as a note taped onto the map. The brand's most personal moment, used in the home hero, on the shortlist page header and in share images.

- A white card with a 1.5px ink border, tilted −1.2°, a strip of sunflower "tape" on top, and the title in `map-note` handwriting.
- Picked cars: the small tick-road mark, the car name in Atkinson bold (always readable), the person's own reason in handwriting.
- Ruled out: an empty box, the name in `ink-3` with a tomato pen strike, the reason in handwriting. Ruling a car out and saying why is part of the honest voice.
- Notes are the user's own words. Suggested reasons ("air-cooled battery") come from our data and are labelled as suggestions on the shortlist page.
- Decoration only where it sits in the margins: the shortlist page itself lists cars as CarCards; the note is its header and share image.
- Reduced motion: no tilt animation (there is none by default; never wobble it).
