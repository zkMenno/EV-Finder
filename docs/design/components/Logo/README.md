# Logo

The tick-road: a shortlist tick drawn as a road, ending at a sunflower destination. Paired with the EV Shortlist wordmark in Overpass ExtraBold, whose i is dotted with the same sunflower disc.

## Parts
- **Mark**: tomato road with an ink edge, a white dashed centre line, and a sunflower disc at the end. The short arm is where you start; the disc is where you're going. One shape says both "picked" and "road trip".
- **Wordmark**: "EV Shortlist", two words, capital E, V and S. Never "EVShortlist", "Ev Shortlist" or all caps. The dot on the i is the sunflower disc with an ink ring.
- **Lockup**: mark left, wordmark right. The disc rises above the cap height like a sun. Spacing: the gap between mark and wordmark equals the wordmark's cap height × 0.3; keep clear space of one disc diameter all round.

## Sizes
- Lockup: 24px tall minimum (header 32px desktop, 28px phone).
- Mark with dashes: 32px and up. Below 32px use the small mark (no dashes): favicon 16px, tab icons 24px.
- App icon: the mark on a `sea` tile with 23% corner radius.

## Colour
- Use the inline SVG with tokens (`var(--ink)`, `var(--tomato)`, `var(--sunflower)`), so it follows the theme. In dark, the ink edge becomes the light ink and reads as a sticker outline.
- Files in the Logo asset group are fixed-colour copies for places tokens don't reach (social, email, press).
- Never recolour the road green or blue, never drop the ink edge, never put the lockup on tomato or sunflower.

## Taglines
- Primary: **Compare EVs. Make your shortlist.**
- Explainer (under the hero): "Real range, real running costs and real used prices for every electric car. Keep the ones that fit."
- Short forms: "Every used EV, compared." / "Your EV shortlist."
