# DetailSwitch

The Simple / Detailed / Nerd switch sets how much detail every page shows.

- Three toggle buttons in a group with `aria-pressed`. The selected one gets a surface background, a sunflower underline and ink text.
- The choice is remembered and written into the URL (`?detail=nerd`), so shared links keep it.
- It lives in the header on desktop and at the top of Explore and car pages on phones.
- Switching adds columns and sections on the right and below. It never reorders what people already see.
- Labels are translated. Keep them one word each, and the buttons grow for longer words.
