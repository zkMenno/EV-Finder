# Affiliate and partner programmes for EV Finder (research as of 2026-11-15)

Legend: 🟢 seen on an official or network page I opened; 🟡 secondary source (directory, aggregator, my own background knowledge); 🔴 not found or unknown. No payout figure below is invented; where nothing was public it says so.

## Lead paragraph (the answer)

**None of the big used-car marketplaces runs a publicly findable affiliate programme for our launch countries.** I found no open programme for AutoScout24 (NL/DE/BE/FR), mobile.de, Marktplaats, 2dehands/2ememain, leboncoin or La Centrale 🔴. Three partial exceptions: (1) AutoScout24 **Switzerland** has an Awin programme (30% per listing, 30-day cookie 🟢), but Switzerland is not a launch country and the programme pays for dealer *listings*, not buyer clicks; (2) mobile.de appears in an old Affilinet-era directory entry (lead payouts up to EUR 1.50, status "Closed") 🟡 that looks stale; (3) **Auto Trader UK** appears to run a programme on CJ Affiliate, but only a third-party directory says so, and the payout shown (GBP 0 to 5 per sale) is thin 🟡. In practice the marketplace money will come from **partner contracts** (the Autonex and Adevinta conversations already planned in the spec), not from a sign-up form. What *does* exist openly, and fits a used-EV finder, are **lead programmes next to the purchase**: car leasing and subscription (Leasing.com UK on Awin and Impact, Auto.nl on Daisycon for NL, FINN on Awin for DE, Carwow on Daisycon/others) and, later, battery tests (no public affiliate programme found for AVILOO, Generational or ClearWatt).

**Best first applications (all free, self-serve):** Awin (account is needed for Leasing.com UK and FINN DE, and it is also the network where AutoScout24 CH sits), Daisycon (Auto.nl NL; Carwow DE), and CJ Affiliate (Auto Trader UK, check it exists). In parallel send the partner pitches to Autonex/AutoScout24, Adevinta (mobile.de, Marktplaats, leboncoin) and Auto Trader UK, because "See listings" links will have no affiliate tag until those succeed. Do not wait on them: the links work untagged.

A caution on the sole-trader point: nothing public requires a limited company, but several networks ask for a business/tax identity (a KvK number and VAT ID suit a Dutch sole trader well) and a live site with real content before approving. Apply once the site is public.

## What deep links look like (important for the whole plan)

Marketplaces are consumer sites, not advertisers, so no network page I opened says "you may link to a pre-filtered search page" or forbids it 🔴. Linking to a public search URL is ordinary web linking; the open question is only whether an *affiliate tag* can be attached to it. Networks that support it (Awin, Daisycon) normally allow a deep link to any page of the merchant domain through a tracking-link generator; Daisycon's Carwow DE page explicitly lists "Sub IDs and deeplinks are supported" 🟢. For the other merchants, deep-link rules are not published 🔴 and must be confirmed in the network's programme terms after approval.

## What to do next (Menno must do these himself)

1. **Create an Awin publisher account** (free; register as a sole trader with KvK number, VAT ID if any, and IBAN). Apply to Leasing.com UK and FINN DE once a live site with real content exists; then use Awin's directory to search for La Centrale, mobile.de and any other merchants I could not find (Awin's merchant list is behind login).
2. **Create a Daisycon publisher account** and apply to Auto.nl (NL) and Carwow DE.
3. **Create a CJ Affiliate account** and search for Auto Trader UK; confirm whether it really exists and what the terms are.
4. **Send the partner pitches** (spec Phase 1): Autonex (AutoScout24), Adevinta (mobile.de, Marktplaats, 2dehands, leboncoin), Auto Trader UK, La Centrale. Ask each: affiliate or lead fee for pre-filtered EV clicks, permitted deep-link format, and feed access.
5. **Email AVILOO, Generational and ClearWatt** asking for a referral arrangement (low priority until "Next").
6. **Re-check every cookie length, rate and brand-bidding rule inside the network dashboards after approval.** Public pages here are mostly secondary and incomplete.
7. Keep affiliate disclosure on every link-out page (labelling "ad/affiliate" and independent ranking statements) to match the "no paid rankings" principle.
