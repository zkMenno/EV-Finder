# Brand name shortlist (research, 15 Nov 2026)

**Recommendation: EV Almanac** (evalmanac.com and evalmanac.nl both unregistered on 15 Nov 2026; .eu not checked). Backups: **EV Roadbook** and **EV Field Guide**.

This is a quick screen, not legal clearance. Nothing was registered or bought. Before any public step, run a proper EUIPO / BOIP / UKIPO / INPI / DPMA search (or ask a trademark attorney) and register the domains the same day.

## Brief

- Descriptive like "EV Database", but clearly not EV Database, EVKX, ev-database.nl, Recurrent or AutoUncle. No maker names, no "Tesla".
- Has to read well in EN, NL, DE, FR, ES and IT.
- Fits the brand identity: a road-trip, hand-drawn holiday or campsite map style; friendly and family-friendly; audience aged 30–70, mostly first-time EV buyers; the voice of a knowledgeable neighbour.

## Why EV Almanac

1. **Descriptive in the EV Database way:** an almanac is a reference book of facts, published again each year. That matches a versioned, sourced catalogue that keeps changing.
2. **Distinct from competitors:** no "database", "data", "index" or "KX" in it, so there's little risk of confusion with EV Database, EVKX or ev-database.nl.
3. **Works in all six languages:** it's a cognate everywhere, with no bad meanings that we found in NL, DE or FR.
4. **Fits the identity:** almanacs were classic illustrated family books (weather, maps, travel), which suits a hand-drawn holiday-map look and a "knowledgeable neighbour" voice.
5. **Domains:** both .com and .nl are unregistered. The .eu still needs checking.

**Backup 1, EV Roadbook:** the strongest road-trip fit and a European loanword, but a small risk of reading as a route planner.
**Backup 2, EV Field Guide:** the clearest "learn to spot the good ones" idea, but it overlaps with crowded "EV Guide" naming.

## Caveats

- **Weak marks:** "EV" plus a common word is descriptive. EUIPO and UKIPO may refuse to register the plain word mark (EUTMR Art. 7(1)(b)/(c)), so protection would most likely come from a logo (figurative) mark. That's normal for names like "EV Database", but it means we can't stop others from using similar words.
- **Registry databases not searched directly:** TMview (tmdn.org) and EURid both refused automated access (robots and a bot challenge). The trademark screen is based on web search only. 🟢 means "nothing found by web search", not "clear in the register".
- **Domain status can change at any time.** RDAP 404 means no registration record. It doesn't tell us about premium pricing or registry reservations.

## Method

- **.com:** `https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/<name>.com` (HTTP status code)
- **.nl:** `https://rdap.sidn.nl/domain/<name>.nl` (HTTP status code)
- **.eu:** IANA's RDAP bootstrap (`https://data.iana.org/rdap/dns.json`) lists no RDAP service for .eu. `whois.eurid.eu` returned a JavaScript bot challenge to curl and was blocked by robots.txt for WebFetch. A direct port-43 WHOIS query was refused by the session's permission system. 🔴 Unverified: check by hand at https://eurid.eu or at a registrar.
- **Trademarks:** WebSearch for each name plus terms like app, trademark and EUIPO. TMview was blocked by robots.txt.
